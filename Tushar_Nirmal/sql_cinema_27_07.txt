CineStream is a fast-rising global OTT platform preparing for the Annual Content Strategy Meeting.

Your findings will directly influence decisions on:

Which movies get featured next quarter
Which genres deserve more investment
Which directors merit a Spotlight Series
How movies rank within their genre
The Head of Content, Finance Lead, and Regional Marketing Managers are counting on your queries.

Question 1
Beginner
Business Context
The Content team is launching Best of English Cinema Post-2020 for subscribers.

Your Task
Retrieve the title and release year of all English language movies released after 2020, sorted by most recent first.

solution:

select m.title,m.release_year 
from movies m 
where m.release_year >2020 and m.language ='English';

Question 2
Intermediate
Business Context
Finance is building a Genre Performance Report across box office and audience ratings.

Your Task
For each genre, find total box office collection and average user rating.

solution:
select m.genre,sum(b.collection_cr) as collection ,avg(r.rating) as average_user_rating
from movies m 
join box_office b on b.movie_id=m.movie_id
join reviews r on r.movie_id=m.movie_id
group by m.genre;


Business Context
CineStream is planning a Director Spotlight Series for directors with more than one movie.

Your Task
Show eligible directors, number of movies, and total box office collection.

solution
with cte as (select d.director_id as did,d.full_name as directors,count(m.movie_id) as number_of_movies,sum(b.collection_cr) as total_box_office 
from movies m 
join directors d on d.director_id=m.director_id
join 
join box_office b on b.movie_id=m.movie_id
group by d.director_id,d.full_name)
select directors,number_of_movies,total_box_office from cte  where number_of_movies>1;

Regional Marketing Managers need a Genre Leaderboard for licensing and ad pitches.

For each movie, show title, genre, collection, genre rank, and percentage of genre total rounded to 2 decimals

with cte as (select m.movie_id as movie,m.title as title,m.genre as genre,sum(b.collection_cr) as collection
from movies m 
join box_office b on b.movie_id=m.movie_id
group by m.movie_id,m.title,m.genre),
cte2 as (
select *,dense_rank()over(partition by genre order by collection desc) as rank,sum(collection) over(partition by genre) as total from cte)
select title,genre,collection,rank,round((collection*100.00/total),2) as pct from cte2;
